Remit simulation

Bounded, revocable spending authority for an agent. Grant a mandate on the left, then attack it on the right. This runs the same algorithm as reference/policy.js — including the K+1 bucket ring whose earlier K-bucket version had a real cap bypass. Nothing here touches a chain.

The mandate

Clock

day 0, 00:00
Arc block timestamps are non-decreasing but not strictly increasing — sub-second blocks share one. So the engine never treats a timestamp as unique or as proof that time advanced. Wind the clock backwards and watch the cap refuse to refill.

Rolling windows — the bucket ring

Blue slots are inside the trailing window and counted. Black is the current sub-period. Faded slots have aged out. The ring holds K+1 slots and counts K+1 buckets, not K: summing only K covers a span that ends at the end of the current bucket, shifted forward by up to one sub-period, so the oldest still-relevant bucket silently dropped out. That was a real bypass — a fuzz test caught it at 1267 > 1000.
